Extended Data Fig. 1 | Nonlinear radial KvN transport on IBM hardware. We test whether probability moves towards the saturated wake amplitude on four ibm_cleveland qubits. A bond-dimension-two matrix product state prepares 16 cells. Four steps (a,b) use all 16,000 shots per circuit and six (c,d) all 32,000. a,c, Probability in the two outermost cells (r ≥ 7/8, centres 29/32 and 31/32). b,d, Mean radius. Dotted lines mark 31/32, the largest possible mean for nonnegative cell probabilities. Mitigation can give larger estimates. Solid lines show ideal circuits and dashed lines continuum means. Bars show one standard error from shot noise for measured circles and one bootstrap standard deviation for mitigated squares (1,000 resamples of both circuits’ counts). Small bars lie inside the markers. See Supplementary Note 14.
