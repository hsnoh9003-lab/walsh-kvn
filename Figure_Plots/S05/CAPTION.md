Supplementary Figure S5 | How starting position affects the mean path under coupled cubic drift. Paths track mean x and y from time 0 to 1 for interior, near-boundary, near-stable (1, 0) and near-unstable (0, 1) inputs. Squares mark every eighth circuit step (64 steps, 32 × 32 cells). Dashed curves average classical trajectories from the same cell probabilities. Arrows give local drift. Insets enlarge the last two paths. Filled and open circles mark stable and unstable points.
