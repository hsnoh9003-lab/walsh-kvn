Run `python -m Figure_Plots.run F3` from the package root. Panel a uses the study markers Wang [7], Meng [8], Zhang [9] and Tiwari [10]. The protected estimates are Katabarwa [20], Akahoshi [45] and Gidney [46]. The vertical “Study boundary” line is at one million physical qubits. The horizontal “Model threshold” line is at a physical error probability of 10⁻². The plotting code reads their citation numbers from data/reference_points.csv.

The method legend identifies the wake calculation for Walsh–KvN and BE–KvN and the one-dimensional sine-drift calculation for LCHS–KvN.
