Fig. 3 | Physical resources for nonlinear ensemble prediction. a, Hardware experiments and estimates for error-corrected designs. The shaded EFTQC window spans 10⁴–10⁶ physical qubits at physical error probabilities of 10⁻⁴–10⁻³. The partially fault-tolerant STAR architecture⁴⁵ marks the lower-left corner. The upper-right corner rounds the 897,864-qubit RSA-2048 estimate⁴⁶ up to one million. The vertical line marks the study boundary (10⁶ physical qubits) and the horizontal line marks the model threshold (10⁻²). b, Minimum-qubit (open) and fastest (filled) designs at a per-attempt error budget of 10⁻⁴. c, Total task time at physical error probability 5×10⁻⁴ and expected time per accepted shot (inset). For the same wake and 1% relative RMS field error, Walsh–KvN requires 815 shots and BE–KvN³⁷ requires 813 accepted shots. LCHS–KvN¹⁹ uses 815 accepted shots for the sine drift. Its fastest design (2.98 million qubits) exceeds the axis limit in b. Axis breaks separate time scales. Supplementary Notes 10, 11 and 13 give the assumptions.
