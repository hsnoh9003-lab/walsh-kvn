Fig. 5 | Nonlinear benchmarks and three-coordinate wake simulation. a, Quadratic (Q2), cubic (C3), quintic (Q5) and two-dimensional coupled cubic (2D C3) drifts. Filled/open fixed points are stable/unstable. Blue, purple and red denote near-stable, interior and near-unstable inputs. Diamonds show shot-noise-free relative moment errors; squares/bars show medians and 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of 5,000 estimates, each with 10,000 shots. b, Initial (grey) and final quintic densities from circuit (blue) and classical trajectories (dashed). x_(0) is the Gaussian centre before truncation to [0,1]. c, Qubits and total task time for coupled cubic input, using 674 shots without a qubit cap. d, Three-coordinate wake means from growth (blue) and late (orange) circuits versus continuum ensembles (dashed). e, Physical resources without a qubit cap. Dashed/open and solid/filled designs minimize qubits and time. In c,e the RMS target is 1% and the error budget is 10⁻⁴. Grey marks qubits outside the EFTQC window.
