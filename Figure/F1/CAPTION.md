Fig. 1 | From wake dynamics to fault-tolerant circuit costs. a, Ensemble spreading near an unstable fixed point and concentration near a stable one. b, Proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) and sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy) yield a three-coordinate polynomial model. c, Four Walsh coefficients represent eight rotation angles that vary linearly with the even-pair index on a 16-cell grid. d, Phase half steps flank the even–odd–even radial rotations. e, Radius and phase readouts yield ensemble-averaged modal coordinates for flow-field reconstruction with the unstable steady-state solution u_(st) and spatial modes φ_(x), φ_(y) and φ_(z). The known phase θ_(c) is restored for the two oscillatory coordinates. f, Circuit and shot counts, the error budget and hardware parameters determine the required number of physical qubits and total task time.
