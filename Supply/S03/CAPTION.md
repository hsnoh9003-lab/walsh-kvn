Supplementary Figure S3 | Separating spatial and Trotterization errors. a, Initial and final wake densities on 64 cells. b, Field bias against the continuum. Dotted lines use exact grid evolution. Rings mark 64 cells with 33 steps and 128 with 66. c, Trotterization error relative to exact evolution on 64 cells. d, Moment errors with grid refinement for quadratic (Q2), cubic (C3), quintic (Q5) and coupled cubic (2D C3) drift. Exact grid moments are compared with trajectories from prepared probabilities spread uniformly within each cell (open) or the target truncated Gaussian (filled). e, Trotterization error on 64 scalar or 16 × 16 coupled cells. We measure the distance between states after removing a common phase difference invisible to measurements. Dotted guides in c,e fall fourfold when step count doubles.
